# Symptom basis

Why the forms ask what they ask. This is the design reasoning, not clinical guidance.

## Both poles, and mixed states

Lodestone started with one theme and widened. Bipolar disorder includes elevated, depressive and mixed presentations, and the same person can show different early signs before different episodes. The forms cover all of them and leave the weighting to the person using it.

DSM-5 symptom lists the check-in detail page maps onto:

| Manic or hypomanic episode | Check-in item |
|---|---|
| Inflated self-esteem or grandiosity | Confidence |
| Decreased need for sleep | Hours slept with Energy (auto sign: less sleep, more energy) |
| More talkative, pressure to keep talking | Talkativeness and social drive |
| Racing thoughts, flight of ideas | Thought speed |
| Distractibility | Concentration |
| Increase in goal-directed activity, agitation | Energy, Anxiety or restlessness |
| Risky activity, spending | Spending or risk urges |
| Elevated, expansive or irritable mood | Mood above baseline, Irritability |

| Major depressive episode | Check-in item |
|---|---|
| Depressed mood | Mood below baseline |
| Loss of interest or pleasure | Interest and enjoyment |
| Appetite or weight change | Appetite |
| Insomnia or hypersomnia | Sleep drift, oversleeping |
| Psychomotor agitation or slowing | Anxiety or restlessness, Thought speed (slowed) |
| Fatigue, low energy | Energy |
| Worthlessness or excessive guilt | Guilt or worthlessness |
| Trouble thinking or concentrating | Concentration |
| Thoughts of death | Thoughts of death or not being here |

Mixed features (several symptoms of the opposite pole during an episode) appear on Pattern as days with signs in more than one tier and on Daily as, for example, high energy next to hopelessness. The stacking view shows it without a separate form.

## Early signs vary by person

Systematic reviews of prodromes report that individual patients differ widely in which signs they notice and how long before an episode they appear. Reported averages run from about one to four weeks for manic prodromes and from about one to four months for depressive ones. Frequently reported signs include increased energy, reduced need for sleep, increased social activity and racing thoughts before mania, and anxious or negative thoughts, low mood, lower energy, social withdrawal and sleep problems before depression. Guilt or worthlessness shows up for some people and not others. That variation is the reason the sign list is the user's, with example rows.

## The spike form

A spike is a sudden strong state with a trigger the person can rate: guilt, despair, irritability, agitation, a surge of energy or ideas, or an urge to spend or take a risk. Intensity minus trigger rating is the gap the Pattern tab tracks. A large gap with nothing to account for it is the pattern the form is built to surface. The kind is a field, so one form serves every direction.
